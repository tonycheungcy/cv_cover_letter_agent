# JD Analysis — Junior Data Scientist (Lane Crawford)

## 3 Critical Responsibilities

1. **Mine, manipulate, and perform statistical analysis on multiple data sets** to build scalable enterprise analytics solutions
2. **Select features, build, and optimize classifiers** using various machine learning techniques
3. **Generate and validate hypotheses with testing methods (A/B, multi-variate)** to measure the impact of ongoing campaigns and retail initiatives

## 5 Essential Skills

1. **Bachelor's / Master's Degree in a relevant field** (Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Business Analytics, Econometrics)
2. **Python or SQL** programming to manipulate data and draw insights from large data sets
3. **Statistical concepts** — distributions, hypothesis testing, and linear regression
4. **Basic machine learning techniques** — clustering, decision trees, with understanding of mechanisms, advantages, and drawbacks
5. **Proficiency in English** (written and verbal)

## Key Business Outcomes

- **Improve business decision-making** through data-driven solutions
- **Measure the impact of ongoing campaigns and retail initiatives** via rigorous experimentation (A/B, multi-variate testing)
- **Build scalable enterprise analytics solutions** that operate on multiple real-time decision systems
- **Optimize retail operations** (inventory, demand, customer behavior) in a luxury department store context
