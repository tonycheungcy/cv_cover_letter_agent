Junior Data Scientist (Welcome Fresh Graduate)

The Ideal Candidate:
- Bachelors / Master's Degree in relevant field (i.e. Mathematics, Statistics, Computer Science, Business Analytics, Econometrics)
- Fresh graduates are welcome to apply
- Basic knowledge on programming language such as Python or SQL to manipulate data and draw insights from large data sets

- Exposure in basic machine learning techniques including clustering, decision trees including knowing the mechanism behind them and real-world advantages/drawbacks
- Optional exposure in basics of CNN and RNN
- Proficient in English
